Lead Story

Newsrooms have years of valuable footage, but too much of it is buried in folders, drives, transcripts, exports, and half-labeled archives.

This toolkit explores how AI-analyzed video metadata can help producers turn a script into visual beats, match existing archive footage, and create responsible prompt candidates when coverage is missing.
