# Responsible AI

This toolkit assumes that newsrooms and production teams need control, consent, and review.

## Principles

- Do not publish generated visuals without editorial approval.
- Do not create anchor avatars without explicit permission.
- Do not use private footage in public examples.
- Do not generate misleading emergency, disaster, crime, or conflict footage.
- Do not imply generated material is real footage.
- Respect rights, licenses, privacy, and local editorial policy.

## Suggested labels

Generated or synthetic visual material should be labeled according to newsroom policy and platform requirements.
