# Sample Local News Script

> Synthetic demo script. Not based on a real broadcast.

## Story Slug
Community Cooling Centers Open During Heat Wave

## Anchor Intro
City officials opened three emergency cooling centers today as temperatures climbed above 105 degrees across the valley.

## Reporter Track 1
Families arrived throughout the afternoon carrying water bottles, small fans, and bags of supplies.

## Reporter Track 2
Public health officials say seniors, outdoor workers, and people without reliable air conditioning are most at risk.

## Reporter Track 3
The city says the centers will remain open through Friday evening, with free transportation available from several bus stops.

## Anchor Tag
Residents can find the nearest cooling center through the city website or by calling 311.
