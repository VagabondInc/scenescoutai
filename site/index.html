SceneScoutAI Open Research Toolkit

A public research scaffold for archive-aware generative media workflows in newsrooms, documentary teams, and production organizations.

This toolkit explores how video archive metadata, script parsing, and fal-compatible generation prompts can help turn a news script into a structured visual package plan.
