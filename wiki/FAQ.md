# FAQ

## Is SceneScoutAI fully open source?

No. This repository is the open research toolkit. The hosted SceneScoutAI product may remain commercial.

## Can I use this commercially?

The toolkit is licensed under Apache 2.0. Review the license and consult your own counsel for commercial use.

## Does this include real newsroom footage?

No. Examples should use synthetic or public-domain data only.

## Can this generate final broadcast packages automatically?

This scaffold is for research and planning. Human editorial review is required.
